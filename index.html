About

Research at the intersection of power, computing, and transportation.

I am a KAUST Global Fellow at the King Abdullah University of Science and Technology. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from The University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Yunhe Hou.

At KAUST, I work with Prof. Charalambos Konstantinou on coordinated planning and operation of AI data centers, transportation energy systems, and power grids.

Before my Ph.D., I worked as an engineer at China Railway Siyuan Survey and Design Group, where I gained engineering experience in transportation-energy infrastructure planning and implementation.

Research

Research themes

My research combines infrastructure modeling, optimization, and machine learning to make emerging energy systems more flexible, secure, and resilient.

01

Grid-Interactive AI Data Centers

Joint planning of computing capacity, grid access, interconnection lead times, workload flexibility, energy resources, and power-security constraints.

02

Electrified Transportation & Infrastructure Resilience

Railway energy systems, flexible traction power supply, extreme-weather resilience, grid-support services, adaptive control, and digital twins.

03

Decision-Making under Uncertainty

Distributionally robust optimization, decision-dependent uncertainty, multi-stage planning, risk-aware decision-making, and resilient operations.

04

Data-Driven Energy Modeling

Diffusion and generative models, causal learning, scenario generation, and uncertainty-aware machine learning for power and infrastructure systems.
