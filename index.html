Research

Research Interests

I study how flexible, data-intensive, and transportation infrastructures can become active participants in secure and low-carbon power systems.

01

Grid-Interactive AI Data Centers

Joint planning and operation of computing capacity, grid interconnection, and energy resources; workload flexibility; power-security constraints; and cross-regional data-center portfolios.

02

Electrified Transportation & Infrastructure Resilience

Railway energy systems, flexible traction power supply, grid-support services, extreme-weather resilience, adaptive operation, and digital-twin-enabled decision-making.

03

Decision-Making under Uncertainty

Distributionally robust optimization, decision-dependent uncertainty, risk-aware planning, and multi-stage optimization for energy and infrastructure systems.

04

Data-Driven Energy Modeling

Diffusion and generative models, scenario generation, causal and uncertainty-aware learning, and machine-learning-enabled representations for power-system planning and operation.
